# Season Recap and archive contribution contract

The first Season Recap is a reproducible editorial projection of reviewed public season signals. It does not consume personal reading history, precise viewing time, wallet addresses, check-ins, device identifiers or location.

`SeasonRecapProjectionService` is connected to `JdbcSeasonRecapSource` through lazy Spring beans. `POST /api/v1/publisher/season-recaps` requires the explicit validated OIDC publisher role and accepts a projection UUID, season UUID, poster asset UUID, as-of timestamp and one to 32 unique canonical `TEAM_SEASON` fact UUIDs. It accepts no metric values or private reader inputs. The supporting season and relationships must already exist in the canonical basketball store, with public-reference-allowed evidence, a current attributable `CONFIRMED` review, and no unresolved contradiction. Reference freshness and rights are checked again on every presentation.

The initial metric is `TEAM_SEASON_COVERAGE_V1`: the number of UTC calendar days shared by a canonical team/season record and the elapsed season interval, divided by elapsed season days. Season end is exclusive; a missing relationship end means the interval continues through the as-of date. The as-of calendar date must be after the season start. This measures the period recorded in selected public facts, not wins, scores, activity, participation quality, or a complete census of teams. Multiple selected records for the same team are rejected. Canonical fact UUID order fixes signal ordinals. The generator derives the seed from the opaque projection UUID and supplies descriptive Traditional Chinese text; callers cannot replace the values or text. All source evidence snapshots, including season evidence, remain explicitly referenced.

V024 stores the fact UUID list and generated payload as one immutable `season_recap_projection`. Retrying the same projection identity and inputs is idempotent; different content under that identity is rejected. The stored fact list provides ordinal-to-canonical-fact lineage, and each immutable fact provides its evidence references. Stored reference and review timestamps must precede the as-of instant. Creation does not publish a recap.

The public output is `season-recap-v1`: at most 32 values between 0 and 1, a fixed signed 32-bit seed, `season-ink` palette, bounded line width, stable IDs, as-of time and evidence snapshot IDs. The generator returns an ordinary `generative-canvas` ContentDocument for the existing article authoring and SSR reader. Draft save/revision, publication approval/worker, public article reads and offline manifests bind every recap block to the exact stored generated payload; hand-written or modified recap JSON is rejected. The check remains independent of ordinary non-recap articles.

`GET /api/v1/public/seasons/{seasonId}/recaps/{projectionId}` returns the document, resolved public poster and canonical article path only after the exact payload appears in a current immutable article publication snapshot, is linked to its poster, and passes the existing public article/issue boundary. Responses use `Cache-Control: no-store`; conditional requests do not bypass fresh evidence or rights checks. A missing, draft, ambiguous, withdrawn or superseded publication is unavailable. `GET /api/v1/me/seasons/{seasonId}/recaps/{projectionId}` requires OIDC authentication and returns 404: this implementation stores no owner-scoped private projections. A login or stamp does not authorize access to another reader's history.

The registry binds each preset to its own parameter validator before loading a static local module. `court-pulse-v1` remains supported. The recap draws deterministic geometry without a clock, global random source, remote code or asset URL. The current rights decision is required again when serializing a stored projection. Withdrawal suppresses every presentation channel; a reader that receives no rights-valid recap poster also suppresses the derived values and canvas. Browser reloads still rely on the existing publication withdrawal API boundary. This cannot recall an already disconnected third-party copy.

Archive contributions support historical photos, tickets and oral histories. Each private record binds a contributor account, asset identity, affirmative consent/time and a rights record with owner, license, channels, collectible/redistribution flags, validity, credit and origin-withdrawal policy. `DRAFT → SUBMITTED → ACCEPTED` is the normal flow; reviewed drafts may be accepted directly. Only a verified publisher may accept; only the contributing reader or a verified publisher may submit/withdraw. The application identity adapter must resolve the actor profile ID from its verified OIDC context. Withdrawal is terminal and appends a sanitized reason-code/actor/time event; historical events are not deleted. Missing consent, mismatched asset rights, expiry or an unapproved channel suppress public output.

The public archive projection contains only contribution ID, kind, asset ID and approved credit/owner/license text. Account identity, consent records, private drafts, rights contracts and event history stay private. Finite and revocable archive rights never authorize IPFS mirroring; `redistributionAllowed` alone does not imply permanent publication permission.

Contracts: `contracts/season-recap.schema.json`, `contracts/archive-contribution.schema.json` and the shared ContentDocument schema. Contract fixtures use invented test identities and public normalized values; they are not real source coverage, participant results or production rights approvals.
